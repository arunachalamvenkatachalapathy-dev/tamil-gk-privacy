Privacy Policy - Tamil GK Auto Publisher

  • Private automation for Arunachalam V to publish Tamil GK videos to his own YouTube and Facebook Pages.
  • Reads videos and metadata from one designated Drive folder. No viewer accounts, trackers or profiles; not designed to collect viewer personal info.
  • Sends the selected video, title, description and metadata to YouTube and Facebook, under their own privacy policies. Nothing is sold.
  • Drive access is read-only. It only lists and downloads from the designated folder, but the drive.readonly permission can technically see other files in that account.
  • Credentials are kept in private automation secrets, never on the site. Temporary video copies are deleted after each run.
  • Limited publication records (question IDs, receipt IDs) are kept in a public GitHub repo, so they may be publicly visible.
  • Contact: nelandar.studio@gmail.com. Page hosted on GitHub Pages.
